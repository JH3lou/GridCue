# Code of Conduct

GridCue follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone who takes part in the project's spaces agrees to it: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any community channel.

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and put the community's well-being ahead of winning an argument. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer, [@JH3lou](https://github.com/JH3lou), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence, and the maintainer applies the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
